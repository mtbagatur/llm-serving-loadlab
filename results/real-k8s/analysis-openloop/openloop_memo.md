## How to read this

- `queue growing` = requests in flight rose by at least 3 (and 20% of the cell) over the arrival window, or median TTFT of the last third of arrivals is at least x2 the first third. Arrivals outpace completions. A heuristic on short cells, not a statistical test.
- Engines with parallel slots (Ollama NUM_PARALLEL, vLLM batching) admit several requests at once, so overload shows up first as more requests in flight and longer total latency, and only later in TTFT.
- Arrivals: seeded Poisson process conditioned on the request count, so the realised rate equals the offered rate while gaps stay bursty. Cells are short (tens of requests): read trends, not single cells.
- Capacity for planning = the highest rate that stays stable under your TTFT SLO, with headroom; not the closed-loop peak throughput.
